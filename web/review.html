Key Match Incidents panel

Five independent reviewers (a former striker, a former defender, a former coach, a league representative and a referees' representative) each read the decision record and vote on two questions: was the decision correct, and was the VAR right to step in or stay out? They never see each other's votes. The measured decision stands; the panel's view is reported exactly as it voted.

Built on synthetic matches with fictional clubs and players. Laws paraphrased from the IFAB Laws of the Game 2026/27; see the official text for the wording.
